About and sources

The map is fully usable offline and shows the OpenStreetMap basemap when online.

Drag to pan. Use the zoom buttons and layer checkboxes to explore the map. The chart runs downstream from left to right. Scroll sideways on a small screen.

Green in the camping row marks public frontage classified for dispersed camping. Tan marks Seneca Nation territory. Small blue ticks mark mapped campgrounds. Other reaches have no mapped public dispersed camping in this atlas.

This is not legal advice. Check current regulations, access and permits with the land manager or the Seneca Nation before camping.

The map shows selected layers from the atlas. Travel estimates use historical flows. Sources include USGS (NHDPlus HR and gage records), USDA Forest Service, Pennsylvania agencies via PASDA, NYSDEC, trail organizations and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
